# SupportSense AI — Demo Banking Support Knowledge Base

## Compromised card or unrecognised card payment
- Mark the ticket as high priority.
- Ask the customer to freeze the card in the banking app if possible.
- Verify the suspicious transaction date, amount, and merchant.
- Create a fraud-dispute case.
- Never ask for a PIN, password, or full card number.

## Cash withdrawal not recognised
- Mark the ticket as high priority.
- Ask for the ATM location, withdrawal date, and amount.
- Verify whether the physical card is still with the customer.
- Create a cash-withdrawal dispute case.

## Card swallowed by an ATM
- Mark the ticket as high priority.
- Ask for the ATM location, bank name, and approximate time.
- Advise the customer to freeze the card.
- Start card replacement guidance if needed.

## Cash withdrawal fee or charge
- Ask for the withdrawal date, ATM location, and fee amount.
- Check whether the ATM was outside the supported network.
- Explain that a review may be needed before a refund.

## Card delivery or card arrival
- Ask for the card order date.
- Check delivery tracking before escalating.
- Do not expose full address information.

## Transfer pending
- Ask for the transfer date, amount, and recipient details.
- Check whether the transfer is pending, failed, or completed.
- Never request passwords or one-time codes.
